Where we go

Uganda's best parks

Uganda packs mountain gorillas, chimpanzees, the big game of the savanna and the source of the Nile into one country. Most of our trips combine two or three of these.

  • South-west Uganda

    Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

    Home to almost half the world's remaining mountain gorillas. An hour with a habituated family.

  • Northern Uganda

    Murchison Falls

    The Nile squeezed through a seven-metre gorge, with elephant, giraffe and lion on the plains below.

  • Western Uganda

    Queen Elizabeth Park

    The Kazinga Channel cruise, Kasenyi game drives and the tree-climbing lions of Ishasha.

  • Western Uganda

    Kibale Forest

    Chimpanzee tracking in one of the densest primate forests in Africa, with red colobus and mangabey on the same trails.

  • Eastern Uganda

    Jinja and the Nile

    Where the Nile leaves Lake Victoria. Tubing through the rapids, and flat water at sunset.

  • Eastern Uganda

    Sipi Falls

    Three waterfalls off the side of Mount Elgon, with arabica coffee gardens and a long view east.

Who we are

A local team, not an agent abroad

Wild Africa Trails has been planning and running trips in Uganda since 2023. We are a Ugandan team, based in the country we guide in rather than booking it from somewhere else. Our guides work in English, Luganda, Swahili and French, along with most of Uganda's local languages - and where a group needs a language we don't cover, we bring in an interpreter.

We handle the parts that are awkward to arrange from abroad: gorilla and chimpanzee permits, park entry fees, transport in our own game drive vehicle, and your accommodation near the parks you are visiting. Send us your dates, your group size and what you would like to see, and you will get back a written itinerary with a clear price.

Where you'll stay

We book the beds too

Your accommodation is arranged as part of the trip, close to the parks you are visiting - lodges and bandas, mosquito nets up, a bed to come back to after an early start. Tell us the standard you want and we will match it to your budget.
